ZAB 128 (129) – MARA NYINGI WAMENITESA

K.D-Mara nyingi wamenitesa, tangu ujana A7wangu;
mara nyingi wamenitesa, na aseme IsraD-eli.
M.+K.Mara nyingi wamenitesa, tangu ujana A7wangu;
mara nyingi wamenitesa, lakini hawakuniD-weza.
M.Wakulima wamelima mgongoni A7mwangu
wamefanya mirefu mifuo D-yao.
Wakulima wamelima mgongoni A7mwangu
wamefanya mirefu mifuo D-yao.
Lakini Bwana aliye mwenye A7haki,
amezikata kamba zao wasio D-haki.
M.+K.Warudi nyuma wanaoichukia SA7ion,
warudi nyuma wanaoichukia SiD-on.
M.Na wawe kama majani ya dA7arini,
yanyaukayo kabla hayajaD-mea.
Ambayo mvunaji haujazi mkono A7wake,
wala mfunga miganda kifua D-chake.
M.+K.Warudi nyuma wanaoichukia SA7ion,
warudi nyuma wanaoichukia SiD-on.
