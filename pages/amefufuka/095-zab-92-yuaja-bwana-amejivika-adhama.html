ZAB 92 (93) – YUAJA BWANA AMEJIVIKA ADHAMA

K.D-Yuaja Bwana, amejivika adG-hama,
amejivika na kujikaza nD-guvu.
M.Ulimwengu u imara usitikiG-sike.
kiti chako ni thabiti, wewe u tangu miD-lele;
utakatifu uifaao nyumba G-yako,
K.utakatifu uifaao nyumba D-yako!
D-Yuaja Bwana, ...
M.D-Kwake atupendaye, na kutuosha dhambi G-zetu,
aliyetufanya ufalme wa makD-uhani,
kwake utukufu na uG-wezo milele na milD-ele,
K.kwake utukufu na uG-wezo milele na milD-ele!
D-Yuaja Bwana, ...
M.D-Tazama yuaja, yuaja na maG-wingu,
tazama yuaja na kila jicho litamD-wona,
hata hao, hata hao waliomcG-homa,
nchi zote za dD-unia.
K.D-Yuaja Bwana, ...
