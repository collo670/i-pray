Daily Mass Readings

November 2025 - Liturgical Year C

15 NOVEMBER 2025 - SATURDAY
Ordinary Weekday/ Optional Memorial of the Blessed Virgin Mary/ Albert the Great, Bishop, Religious, Doctor
First Reading: Wisdom 18: 14-16; 19: 6-9
"For while all things were in quiet silence, and the night was in the midst of her course, Thy almighty word leapt down from heaven from thy royal throne, as a fierce conqueror into the midst of the land of destruction, With a sharp sword carrying thy unfeigned commandment, and he stood and filled all things with death, and standing on the earth, reached even to heaven. For the whole creature was fashioned again in its nature, serving the peculiar commandments that were given to them, that thy children might be kept without hurt. For they saw the cloud overshadowing the camp, and the dry land rising out of the water where before was water: and a way without hindrance out of the Red Sea, and a grassy plain out of the great deep."
Psalm: Psalms 105: 2-3, 36-37, 42-43
"Sing to him, yea sing praises to him: relate all his wondrous works. Glory ye in his holy name: let the heart of them rejoice that seek the Lord. And he slew all the firstborn in their land: the firstfruits of all their labour. And he brought them out with silver and gold: and there was not among their tribes one that was feeble. Because he remembered his holy word, which he had spoken to his servant Abraham. And he brought forth his people with joy, and his chosen with gladness."
Alleluia: Second Thessalonians 2: 14
"Whereunto also he hath called you by our gospel, unto the purchasing of the glory of our Lord Jesus Christ."
Gospel: Luke 18: 1-8
"And he spoke also a parable to them, that we ought always to pray, and not to faint, saying: There was a judge in a certain city, who feared not God, nor regarded man. And there was a certain widow in that city, and she came to him, saying: Avenge me of my adversary. And he would not for a long time. But afterwards he said within himself: Although I fear not God, nor regard man, Yet because this widow is troublesome to me, I will avenge her, lest continually coming she weary me. And the Lord said: Hear what the unjust judge saith. And will not God revenge his elect who cry to him day and night: and will he have patience in their regard? I say to you, that he will quickly revenge them. But yet the Son of man, when he cometh, shall he find, think you, faith on earth?"
